## Project overview

**Brand:** Ahmed Ali Finishing and Décor<br />
**Sector:** Residential finishing and décor<br />
**Jana's role:** Content-idea development, copywriting, visual direction, and planning an educational post series<br />

Finishing is a high-cost decision that depends on details clients may not consider until it is too late. The content is designed to reach them earlier: explaining what to plan, making expertise tangible, and opening a more useful conversation with the service provider.

## Context

- **Business objective:** Establish expertise before the client begins execution.
- **Market opportunity:** Turn recurring finishing questions into lasting marketing assets.
- **Priority audience:** Homeowners planning a finishing project or renovation.
- **Main constraint:** Speak clearly to clients before a problem becomes expensive.

## Strategic direction

1. **Choose decision moments:** Bathroom planning, plumbing, and TV walls—topics that affect comfort and cost after handover.
2. **Problem-before-solution format:** A direct headline gets attention, followed by practical guidance and an invitation to consult early.
3. **Brand consistency:** Unify visual cues and give advice in a practical tone that improves recall and trust.
4. **Conversion readiness:** Design posts to support saves, shares, and enquiry responses, while also creating talking points for the sales call.

## Deliverables

- An educational post series about bathroom planning, plumbing, and TV walls.
- Headlines and copy that can expand into short videos, stories, and mini-guides.
- A measurement path that links content to conversations and site-visit requests.

## Additional content formats

The campaign expanded the content system beyond static guidance, using finishing-quality, electrical-planning, and concealed-plumbing visuals to give homeowners more practical entry points into the brand.

## Measurement plan

- Awareness: Reach and video views.
- Engagement: Saves, shares, and meaningful comments.
- Interest: Messages and service enquiries.
- Conversion: Site-visit requests and bookings where tracking is available.

## Result

The gallery includes a bathroom-planning guide, planning the TV wall before finishing, and preventing plumbing problems early. Before naming any numeric result, the page owner, publishing period, post links, and resulting enquiries or site-visit requests must be confirmed.
