## Project overview

**Brand:** Ahmed Ali Finishing and Décor<br />
**Sector:** Residential finishing and décor<br />
**Jana's role:** Brand identity, page setup, social-media strategy, content strategy, creative direction, Arabic copywriting, design, Reels concepts, publishing, and performance review<br />

Ahmed Ali began with a blank social-media page: no established visual system, no content pillars, no recurring series, and no defined voice. The objective was never simply to fill a feed with attractive interiors. It was to build a recognisable content brand that helps homeowners understand the thinking behind quality finishing.

## Starting point

The first question was not “What should we post?” but “What should this brand be known for?” The page needed to feel clean, premium, architectural, and professional—without becoming distant from homeowners who are making expensive decisions and may not know the technical language of finishing.

I created the visual identity and the social-media environment around it: logo usage, typography, colour, layout, image treatment, information hierarchy, and a consistent way for every post to feel part of one brand. Consistency was designed as a system, not a template. Each topic could look different while still belonging to Ahmed Ali.

## Audience insight

Homeowners do not usually want a lesson in finishing. They want to avoid decisions they will regret after move-in. Their questions are practical: What is hidden behind the wall? What needs to be decided before it is too late? What am I really paying for? Which detail will become expensive to change later?

That insight shaped the page. Technical information became useful stories, common mistakes became hooks, customer questions became educational assets, and invisible workmanship became something the audience could see and understand.

## Content system

The strategy developed around repeatable series rather than disconnected posts.

1. **المستخبي — What is hidden:** Reveals the workmanship that disappears behind walls, under floors, beneath paint, and inside installations. Electrical routes, waterproofing, drainage, AC preparation, and surface preparation became visual proof of where quality actually lives.
2. **اعرف قبل ما تشطب — Know before you finish:** Helps people make decisions while they can still act on them: TV placement, sockets, internet points, kitchen preparation, lighting, and AC requirements.
3. **غلطة — The mistake:** Uses realistic finishing errors as the start of a helpful story: mistake, consequence, explanation, and the better decision to make earlier.

The result is a system where one customer problem can become an educational post, a hidden-detail visual, a mistake story, a decision post, an interactive question, a Reel concept, and a brand message.

## Visual storytelling and AI-assisted production

The visual does more than make a post attractive. It explains part of the idea. A wall is not only a wall; it can reveal the electrical planning inside it. A bathroom image can show why the drainage layer below the surface matters. An AC unit can become a story about installation details.

AI is used as an execution tool when a specific visual is difficult to source through photography. The creative direction still begins with the audience, the decision, the hook, the scene, and the brand. Outputs are selected, refined, and integrated into the final content system—not treated as the strategy itself.

## Content cycle and performance learning

Publishing is not the final step. Content is reviewed through reach, video views, interactions, audience activity, and messaging actions to identify what earns attention, what creates useful interaction, which format deserves another test, and what should change in the next cycle.

**Create → Publish → Measure → Learn → Improve**

## The outcome

Ahmed Ali moved from a blank social-media page to a content brand with a clear standard of communication: not simply “look at our finished work,” but “understand the thinking behind our work.” The content demonstrates expertise through planning, details, correct execution, and long-term thinking—showing the standard instead of only claiming it.
