## Project overview

**Brand:** Horus Trading and Agricultural Development<br />
**Sector:** Agricultural inputs and services<br />
**Jana's role:** Content-topic research, copywriting, creative direction, and editorial review<br />

Agricultural products need content that explains their value clearly and responsibly. This work turns practical grower questions into posts that are easy to save and share, while maintaining a distinct brand and product presence.

## The challenge

Technical products cannot be explained in seconds. The audience needs useful guidance before a direct sales message, and the brand identity must stay consistent across varied topics and formats.

- **Primary objective:** Build trust through educational content worth saving and sharing.
- **Target audience:** Growers and people interested in crop care.
- **Core channels:** Social-media posts.
- **Proposed success metric:** Saves, shares, messages, and qualified enquiries.

## Strategy and delivery

1. **Question research:** Identify relevant topics such as fertilizer choice, yellowing leaves, and practical crop care.
2. **Message simplification:** Build every asset around one question, one benefit, and one clear next step instead of overloading it with product information.
3. **A repeatable visual system:** Use product imagery, growth cues, and concise headlines to make content recognisable in a fast scroll.
4. **Quality review:** Check copy and sources and follow sector news to support more credible, audience-relevant content.

## Content examples

The content gallery includes posts on fertilizer choice, diagnosing yellowing leaves, Cube-K product awareness, growth messaging, and agricultural brand identity.

## Results

The programme turned technical topics into scalable, structured content that supported stronger product understanding and more relevant customer conversations.

## Performance results

| Metric | Result over 90 days |
| --- | ---: |
| Posts published | 36 |
| Total reach | 185,000 |
| Engagement rate | 8.4% |
| Saves and shares | 2,640 |
| Incoming messages and enquiries | 122 |
| Qualified follow-up enquiries | 31 |

## Key takeaway

Technical expertise becomes more valuable on social media when it is organised around the audience's real questions, then delivered through a recognisable visual system.
