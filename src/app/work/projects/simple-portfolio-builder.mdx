## Project overview

**Brand:** Horus Trading and Agricultural Development<br />
**Sector:** Agricultural inputs and services<br />
**Jana's role:** Page creation, social-media strategy, content planning, agricultural content, creative direction, Arabic copywriting, design, publishing, page management, and performance learning<br />

Horus brought together two parts of my background: Agriculture and content creation. The brand had an existing logo but no established social-media presence. My role was to turn that identity into a page with a clear visual direction, a useful tone of voice, and a content system that could speak about Sunny Fertilizer and Cube-K without becoming a repetitive product catalogue.

## The challenge

The product scope was focused, but the audience’s needs were much wider. A grower is not opening social media looking for another product sheet. They are thinking about their crop, what they are noticing, what might be out of balance, and which decision will help them get a better result.

The content needed to make each product relevant through real agricultural context. The approach moved from “here is the product and what it does” to “here is what is happening with the crop, why it matters, and where the product fits.”

## Understanding before simplifying

Agricultural information must be understood before it can be made simple. The process begins with the agricultural logic: what the plant needs, the problem or misconception, the crop signal, the timing, and the expected outcome. From there, the content angle, hook, explanation, visual, and product role can be designed accurately.

**Agricultural understanding → Audience question → Clear hook → Simple accurate explanation → Visual concept → Relevant product**

The aim is not to make the science less accurate. It is to make the science easier to understand and more useful in a fast-moving social-media environment.

## Content system

The page gives different pieces of content different jobs:

- Educational content that explains a crop need, problem, or concept.
- Problem-awareness and misconception content that starts a useful conversation.
- Sunny Fertilizer and Cube-K storytelling that places products in real agricultural context.
- Brand content that builds familiarity with Horus and its agricultural point of view.
- Interactive questions that turn audience responses into insight for the next content cycle.

One product can become an educational post, a problem-awareness visual, a technical explanation, a product-focused story, a comparison, an interactive question, or a short-form video concept. The product stays the same; the audience need and the conversation change.

## Visual direction and AI-assisted production

The visual system was designed to work across education, product communication, and brand storytelling without making every post look the same. Plants, roots, fruit quality, crop signals, and product packs all carry a role in explaining the message—not simply filling the layout.

AI-assisted visuals are evaluated through an agricultural lens. A plant, symptom, or crop relationship must make technical sense before it is used. The tool helps execute a specific creative direction; agricultural understanding guides the selection and refinement.

## Content cycle and performance learning

The page is managed as a living system. Publishing creates information about which questions attract attention, which hooks earn a pause, which formats create interaction, and which ideas deserve another variation. Those signals feed the next plan.

**Plan → Create → Publish → Measure → Learn → Adjust**

## The outcome

Horus became more than a channel for two products. It became a social-media presence where agricultural knowledge, useful storytelling, Arabic copywriting, and a consistent visual direction work together. The content helps growers understand the context first—then see where Sunny Fertilizer and Cube-K belong in the conversation.
