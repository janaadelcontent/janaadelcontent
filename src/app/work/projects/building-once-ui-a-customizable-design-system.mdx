## Project overview

**Organisation:** ELADL MEDIA CO.<br />
**Jana's role:** Operations manager, content writing and publishing, quality management, and reporting<br />
**CV period:** February 2019 – present<br />

Serving several accounts requires more than good ideas. It needs a system that gets the right idea out at the right time with a consistent standard of quality. This example shows how operations management creates client value: clearer output, more reliable reviews, and content decisions informed by market signals.

## Operational challenge

- Multiple accounts, requirements, and approvals while maintaining a consistent quality standard.
- Turning industry news and social signals into relevant topics for each brand.
- The need for reports that help the team and client see what has been delivered and what needs improvement.

## Approach

1. **Workflow organisation:** Assign responsibilities, track delivery, and prepare clear team reports.
2. **Content research:** Use social listening, independent sources, and industry-news monitoring to identify relevant topics and ideas.
3. **Production and review:** Write and publish content, then edit and review it for quality and consistency.
4. **Continuous improvement:** Turn feedback and approved performance data into better topics, copy, and publishing cadence.

## Client value

- An operational point of contact that connects strategy with day-to-day delivery.
- More consistent editorial quality across content types and accounts.
- Reports that make review and decision-making easier rather than simply handing over posts.
- A practical foundation for scaling production without losing brand identity or audience relevance.

## Performance results

| Metric | Result over one quarter |
| --- | ---: |
| Active accounts under management | 4 |
| Content assets produced and published | 96 |
| On-time delivery | 94% |
| First-round approval | 82% |
| Average review rounds per asset | 1.4 |
| Periodic reports delivered | 100% |

## Key takeaway

Structured operations connect strategy to delivery, helping client accounts maintain editorial quality and scale their content output without losing audience relevance.
